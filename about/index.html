About and method

Scentral is a reference for perfumes and the perfumes they inspire: 12,026 perfumes with a full file and 5,118 early reads from 748 houses, and 231 houses followed release by release.

Original and inspired

Original houses create their own scents. Inspired houses make perfumes inspired by famous ones, and many of them create originals too. A perfume from an inspired house that Parfumo members link to an original says “inspired by” on its page; the original lists its inspired versions.

Scent DNA

Every perfume gets a profile built from:

  • How Parfumo members vote on its scent: fresh, sweet, woody, smoky and so on
  • The families its notes belong to
  • The notes themselves

Comparing 2 profiles gives a match from 0 to 100. Perfumes with too few votes use their main accords in place of the votes, and their pages say so.

Three labels, from sure to loose

Inspired by means Parfumo members link the two: they vote that one smells like the other, with moderate (30+ votes) or strong (100+) agreement.

Matches means there is no community link, but the original is a 95+ scent DNA match. The cut-off comes from a check on inspired perfumes that do have a community link: at 95 or more, the closest original is the linked one 56% of the time (174 perfumes). At 90 to 94, it is 20% (229 perfumes).

Closest in style means the closest original is a 88 to 94 match. That is a fair guide to what it smells like, not a claim about what the house had in mind.

The twist

Each pair gets one sentence on what the inspired perfume changes: whether it leans warmer, fresher, sweeter or drier by community votes, which scent family grows, which notes it adds and drops, and how long it lasts next to the original. It reads the listed notes, so a note missing from Parfumo’s list can make a difference look bigger than it is.

Early reads

New perfumes often have notes listed but too few ratings for a full file. Their pages are early reads: the match uses the notes and their families alone, they only ever get the “closest in style” label, and they never count as twins.

Twins

Twins are perfumes from different inspired houses with a DNA match of 96 or more; a family is every bottle connected through such matches. When neither perfume has community votes yet, a twin needs 97. In a test on 2,377 perfumes that have both votes and accords, 96 got the twin verdict right 61% of the time when both sides used accords, and 97 got it right 84% of the time.

Which perfumes count

For the 231 houses Scentral follows release by release, Parfumo lists many perfumes with no release year, and some with impossible ones, like a 1990 date for a house that started in 2020. So the site only shows a followed house’s perfumes dated from the year it started: the founding year where known, otherwise the earlier of Fragrantica’s earliest edition and the earliest year on Parfumo’s own house list. A well-known perfume Parfumo never dated, with 100+ community ratings, stays without a year.

Performance percentiles

Pages never show Parfumo’s scores. They show percentiles: “lasts longer than 84%” means its average longevity rating beats 84% of the 7,526 perfumes with 30+ longevity and projection ratings. Beast mode means the top 10% for both.

Where the data comes from

Notes, community votes and “smells like” links come from Parfumo, and every perfume page links back to its Parfumo entry for reviews, photos and ratings. Scentral’s crawler identifies itself as ScentralBot, follows Parfumo’s robots.txt and waits at least 2 seconds between requests. Scentral shows only results it works out itself. It doesn’t copy reviews, scores or bottle photos.

What the site stores about you

Nothing. There are no accounts, cookies or analytics. The site serves its own fonts, so your browser doesn’t contact a font service, and search runs in your browser. The quiz keeps your answers in the page address, so you can share a result and no server ever sees it.

Who runs it

Scentral is a hobby project, not affiliated with Parfumo or with any fragrance house. Brand and perfume names belong to their owners. Arabic names appear as the houses list them; meanings in the name decoder are short literal translations.
