Method and caveats

An inspired version counts when Parfumo members link it to the original with moderate (30+ votes) or strong (100+) agreement, it comes from an inspired house, and it came out no earlier than the original. Inspired houses are the ones listed in Scentral’s configuration; many also create originals of their own.

Scent DNA combines community scent-type votes (65%), the families of the listed notes (15%) and the notes themselves (20%), scaled from 0 to 100. Perfumes with too few votes use their main accords instead. On perfumes that have both, the accords version puts the right neighbour in its top 5 80% of the time.

Twins are 96%+ DNA matches between different inspired houses (97%+ when neither perfume has community votes yet), grouped when they chain together. Release counts come from the catalogue pages of the 231 houses Scentral follows; a perfume counts only when dated on or after its house’s start.

Ratings are averages of community scores, not measurements, and inspired and original perfumes attract different raters. Percentiles compare each perfume with all 7,526 perfumes that have 30+ longevity and projection ratings.

Notes, community votes and “smells like” links come from the Parfumo community. Scentral works out which perfumes inspired which, the twins, the percentiles and the decoded names. It isn’t affiliated with Parfumo or any fragrance house, and brand and perfume names belong to their owners.
